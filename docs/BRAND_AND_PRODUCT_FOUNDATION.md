# Cash Prophet — Brand & Product Foundation

## IMPORTANT

This document is the **single source of truth** for the Cash Prophet brand, messaging, positioning and product philosophy.

All future website copy, product design, onboarding, documentation, marketing and advertising should align with these principles.

If there is ever a conflict between this document and newly generated content, **this document takes precedence**.

Cash Prophet is not selling calculations.

Cash Prophet is selling **confidence through a trusted financial position**.

---

## Purpose

To remove financial uncertainty and mental burden by giving business owners one trusted financial number that shows where their business really stands.

---

## Mission

To help business owners understand the financial health of their business, reduce financial stress and make better decisions through one trusted financial number.

---

## Vision

A world where every business owner can instantly understand the financial position of their business without relying on an unreliable bank balance, spreadsheets or mental arithmetic.

---

## Core Belief

**Every business deserves one financial number it can trust.**

This belief sits at the centre of the Cash Prophet brand.

Everything else exists to support this belief.

---

## Product Philosophy

Most business owners judge their business using their bank balance.

The problem is that a bank balance is heavily influenced by timing.

Customer payments, VAT, payroll, supplier invoices and other financial commitments constantly distort the picture.

A bank balance tells you how much money is in your account.

It does not reliably tell you where your business really stands.

Cash Prophet continuously accounts for meaningful financial commitments to calculate one trusted financial number that better reflects the financial position of the business.

---

## Product Positioning

Cash Prophet is software that continuously calculates your business's financial position by accounting for meaningful financial commitments.

It produces one trusted financial number called the **Cash Prophet Balance**.

The purpose of the Cash Prophet Balance is to help business owners understand where their business really stands.

Cash Prophet is **not** bookkeeping software.

Cash Prophet is **not** an accounting package.

Cash Prophet is **not** a replacement for accountants.

Cash Prophet is **not** a replacement for detailed cash flow forecasting.

Cash Prophet **complements** accounting software by providing a continuously updated financial position that is easy to understand every day.

---

## Cash Prophet Balance

The Cash Prophet Balance is Cash Prophet's calculation of the business's financial position.

Unlike a bank balance, it already reflects accrued financial commitments.

It is designed to become the trusted financial number a business owner checks every day.

Its purpose is to remove the noise created by lumpy outgoing costs so that business owners can monitor the genuine trend of their business over time.

---

## Who Cash Prophet Is Designed For

Cash Prophet is designed primarily for businesses with **relatively consistent income** throughout the month.

Examples include:

- Leisure businesses
- Hospitality
- Gyms
- Children's activity providers
- Childcare providers
- Subscription businesses
- Service businesses with recurring revenue
- Businesses with regular weekly or monthly income

For these businesses, the Cash Prophet Balance provides a consistent measure of financial position because income and expenditure become comparable throughout the month.

---

## Businesses Where Cash Prophet Balance Is Less Suitable

Businesses with highly irregular, project-based or seasonal income should **not** be positioned as the primary audience for the Cash Prophet Balance.

Examples include:

- Construction
- Property development
- Project-based agencies
- Businesses relying on occasional large contracts
- Businesses with highly unpredictable income

These businesses often require detailed cash flow forecasting because the timing of income is itself a major financial risk.

**Reserve Planner** remains valuable for these businesses because it helps save towards predictable future costs, but Cash Prophet Balance should **not** be marketed as replacing proper cash flow forecasting.

---

## Reserve Planner

Reserve Planner helps businesses continuously save towards predictable future costs.

It allows businesses to prepare for future liabilities without requiring multiple bank accounts or manual "jam jar" budgeting.

Reserve Planner provides value regardless of income pattern.

---

## Tone of Voice

Cash Prophet should always sound:

- Calm
- Reassuring
- Honest
- Intelligent
- Practical
- Human
- Approachable
- Trustworthy

Avoid hype.

Avoid exaggerated claims.

Avoid unnecessary accounting jargon.

Explain complicated concepts simply.

Never imply that accounting is wrong.

Never imply that accountants are unnecessary.

Never claim to have invented accounting.

Instead explain that Cash Prophet applies established accounting principles in a practical way to give business owners one trusted financial number.

---

## Preferred Language

Prefer:

- One trusted financial number
- Where your business really stands
- Financial health
- Financial position
- Financial confidence
- Financial clarity
- Meaningful financial commitments
- Trusted financial position

Use **"underlying financial position"** only when explaining technical concepts.

For customer-facing copy, prefer:

**"Where your business really stands."**

---

## Avoid These Phrases

Avoid:

- True Balance
- Revolutionary accounting
- Replaces accountants
- Replaces accounting software
- Replaces cash flow forecasting
- Magic
- Hidden algorithm
- AI-powered finance
- The only financial number you need

Cash Prophet provides the financial number business owners will check every day.

It does **not** replace every other financial metric.

---

## Brand Promise

Cash Prophet gives business owners one trusted financial number that shows where their business really stands.

It replaces uncertainty with confidence by continuously accounting for meaningful financial commitments, allowing business owners to stop relying on an unreliable bank balance and stop carrying their finances around in their head.

---

## Guiding Principle

Whenever creating features, copy, interfaces, videos, documentation or marketing, always ask:

> Does this increase the business owner's confidence in where their business really stands?

If the answer is no, it does not align with the Cash Prophet brand.

**Confidence is always the outcome.**  
**Trust is always the mechanism.**  
**Clarity is always the experience.**
