# Homepage / primary ad video

**Library key:** `homepage`  
**Target length:** 60–90 seconds  
**Wire URL in:** [`src/content/videos.ts`](../src/content/videos.ts) → `VIDEO_LIBRARY.homepage.url`  
**Appears on:** homepage band `#see-cash-prophet` (and reuse as primary ad creative)

## Goal

Give a business owner enough confidence to try the demo or start free — without claiming to replace accounting or forecasting.

## Brand checks

- Ownable term: **Cash Prophet Balance**
- Enemy: unreliable **bank balance** (not accountants / bookkeeping tools)
- Audience: relatively consistent / recurring income
- Tone: calm, practical, no hype

## Shot list

| # | Visual | Approx. |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | Bank balance looking “fine”; owner uneasy / mental checklist vibe | 0–12s |
| 2 | On-screen equation: bank − spoken for + expected = **Cash Prophet Balance** | 12–28s |
| 3 | Monthly accruing bills — purple row washes partially filled, descending | 28–42s |
| 4 | Due bills + expected receipts (brief) | 42–52s |
| 5 | Reserve planner sawtooth — planned balance + bill drops | 52–68s |
| 6 | Cash Prophet Balance trend ending on today’s figure | 68–78s |
| 7 | CTA card: Start free / See how it works | 78–90s |

Use one coherent demo workspace throughout (same numbers as the share image where possible: bank £42,500 → Cash Prophet Balance £21,976).

## Voiceover script (~80 seconds)

**[Bank balance on screen]**  
Your bank balance only shows what’s sitting in the account. It doesn’t show rent, wages, VAT and the rest already spoken for.

**[Equation builds]**  
Cash Prophet continuously accounts for those commitments, and gives you one trusted number: your Cash Prophet Balance — where your business really stands.

**[Accruing rows]**  
Regular monthly costs build a little every day, so they’re already reflected before payday.

**[Due + receipts]**  
What’s ready to pay, and money you’re confident is coming in, sit in one place — so the number stays honest.

**[Reserve sawtooth]**  
Larger bills that don’t hit every month are funded steadily into a reserve, instead of landing as a surprise.

**[Trend]**  
Each time you save balances, you see whether your Cash Prophet Balance is moving up or down over time.

**[CTA]**  
Less guessing. More confidence. Start free, or see how it works with a live demo.
